## Recommended Listing Setup

1. Use Afternic / GoDaddy List for Sale as the main distribution channel.
2. Add Sedo as a secondary listing channel where there is no conflict.
3. Put Buy Now prices on sub-USD-10,000 names; Afternic/GoDaddy guidance says priced domains convert better than unpriced listings.
4. Use Fast Transfer only after confirming renewal, ownership, lock/protection, and email access.
5. Do not point active business domains to sales landers.
6. For `4quadro.com` + `4quadro.net`, sell as a package first; only separate if a buyer wants `.com` alone at a strong price.
